SMS Messaging Program

Effective date: July 13, 2026

Hodge & Williams ("H&W," "we," "us") is a trade name operated by CD Williams (sole proprietor). We operate an automated SMS auto-reply service. This page describes the program and the terms that apply when you message us.

How the program works

When you send a text message to our business phone number, our system automatically sends you one reply message acknowledging receipt. We do not send marketing, promotional, appointment, or recurring messages.

How to opt in

You opt in by sending a text message to our business number. By texting us, you consent to receive one automated reply in response. We never add you to any list and never message you unless you have first messaged us.

Program disclosures
  • Program name: Hodge & Williams SMS Auto-Reply
  • What you get: One automated reply for each message you send us.
  • Message frequency: One reply per inbound message. No recurring messages.
  • Cost: Message and data rates may apply, as determined by your mobile carrier.
  • Opt out: Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages.
  • Help: Reply HELP for assistance.
  • More: See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Privacy

No mobile information (including your mobile phone number and message content) will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties. See our Privacy Policy for full details.
