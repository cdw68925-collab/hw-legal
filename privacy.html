Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 13, 2026

Hodge & Williams ("H&W," "we," "us") operates automated SMS messaging services, including an SMS auto-reply service. This policy explains what information we collect through that service and how we handle it.

Information we collect

When you send a text message to one of our business phone numbers, we collect your mobile phone number and the content of the message you sent, in order to send you an automated reply acknowledging receipt.

How we use it

We use your phone number and message content solely to send you a reply and to maintain an internal log of message activity for service reliability and troubleshooting. We do not use this information for marketing, advertising, or any purpose unrelated to responding to your message.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your mobile phone number or message content with third parties for marketing purposes. Your information may be processed by our messaging service provider (Twilio Inc.) solely to deliver messages on our behalf, under Twilio's own confidentiality obligations.

Message frequency

You will receive one automated reply message for each message you send to our number. We do not send unsolicited or recurring messages.

Message and data rates

Message and data rates may apply, as determined by your mobile carrier.

Opt-out

You may reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages from us. Reply HELP for assistance.

Data retention

We retain message logs only as long as necessary for service operation and troubleshooting.
