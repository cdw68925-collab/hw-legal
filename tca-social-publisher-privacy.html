Privacy Policy – H&W TCA Social Publisher

Effective date: October 31, 2026

Hodge & Williams ("H&W," "we," "us") is a trade name operated by CD Williams (sole proprietor). We operate a Meta app named "H&W TCA Social Publisher." The app is used only by H&W, on behalf of Tsalach Christian Academy ("TCA"), to publish TCA's approved school-community posts to TCA's own Facebook Page and Instagram professional account on a set schedule. This policy explains what the app accesses and how it is handled. It applies to this app only; our separate SMS privacy policy covers our text-message service.

What the app does

The app schedules and publishes photos and captions that TCA has already approved, and then reads each published post back from Facebook or Instagram to confirm it appeared correctly. It is not a login, sign-up or social-sharing feature for visitors, and members of the public do not use or interact with the app.

Information the app accesses

  • The identifiers of TCA's Facebook Page and Instagram professional account.
  • The posts the app itself publishes there (image, caption, link, publish time, and the post's public link and ID).
  • An access token that authorizes the app to publish to those two accounts. The token is kept only in a protected credential store of our workflow-automation service, never in our files, source code or messages.

Information the app does not collect

The app does not collect, read or store personal information about people who follow, visit, like, comment on or message TCA's Page or Instagram account, and it does not read private messages. It does not use cookies or tracking technologies and does not collect information from your Facebook or Instagram profile.

How we use it

We use this information only to publish TCA's approved posts, confirm they published, keep a record of what was published when, and notify us if a post fails. We do not use it for advertising, profiling, or any unrelated purpose.

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell this information or share it with third parties for their own purposes. It is processed on our behalf by Meta (Facebook and Instagram), our workflow-automation service (n8n Cloud), Microsoft OneDrive (storage of the approved images and publishing records), and Google Gmail (status emails to us). Information may be disclosed if required by law.

Data retention and deletion

We keep the publishing records and approved images for as long as we operate the service for TCA and as needed for troubleshooting. The access token is deleted when the service ends or when access is revoked. Because the app does not collect personal information about members of the public, there is no individual user data to delete; you may still ask us to confirm this or to remove anything you believe we hold by contacting us below, and we will respond promptly. TCA or its administrators can end the app's access at any time by removing it in Facebook's Business Integrations settings or in Instagram's authorized apps settings.

Changes

If this app's data practices change, we will update this page and its effective date.
